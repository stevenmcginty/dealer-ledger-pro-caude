Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026. This policy covers Dealer Ledger Pro and the customer messaging service operated by Radlett Cars, Radlett, Hertfordshire, UK.

What we collect

When you contact us by WhatsApp, SMS, email or through a vehicle listing site we receive your name, phone number, email address and the content of your messages, including the vehicle you enquired about.

How we use it

To answer your enquiry, arrange viewings and test drives, provide quotes and follow up on your enquiry. Replies may be drafted with the help of automated software and are reviewed by our staff. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Service providers that process messages on our behalf: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Google (Gmail, Firebase hosting and database, AI services) and, where enabled, Twilio (SMS). Each acts under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and meet our legal obligations, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by messaging radlettcars@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any WhatsApp or SMS message to opt out of further messages.
